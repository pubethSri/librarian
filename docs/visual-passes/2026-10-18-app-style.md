# Visual pass — App style (Gold + Blue on Stone, diamond tiles, queue series bar)

- **Status:** ⬜ Pending — your pass, on every screen you use
- **Feature:** STATUS.md → "v0.4 reading queue — built" (queue polish (c), your pick 2026-10-18); branch `feature/v0.4-queue`
- **Setup:** on the laptop run `node .claude/dev-test-db.mjs --host` (scratch DB, port 5174), sign in on each device at `http://<laptop-IP>:5174` (PC: `http://localhost:5174`). The scratch queue still holds the books from your queue pass. You chose the style on the OLED laptop; OLED shows faint lines and dark tones differently from LCD / VA panels, so the point of this pass is **each device on its own**: iPad, phone, PC on the VA monitor (and the laptop once more for comparison).

**On each device:**

1. **Tiles** — Dashboard (`/`) and Field → Find: the background is Stone (a very light warm grey) with faint diamond lines. _Too strong:_ the lines catch your eye while reading. _Too faint:_ you can't find them at all at arm's length — both are a one-number change (3% now). The VA monitor (higher contrast, less uniform at the edges) is the likeliest place for them to look stronger; note it.
2. **Cards stay white** — on the dashboard tiles, the series page stat tiles and volume table, the Books table, Bookfairs, Insights charts, Console panels: every card / table / list is white with no lines behind its text. _Red flag:_ diamonds visible behind text or numbers anywhere (tell me the page).
3. **Sidebar** (PC / iPad landscape) — white with even fainter diamonds; the current page has a thin **gold** bar on its left. The collapsed sidebar and the phone's slide-out menu show the same bar.
4. **Phone Queue tab** — **Reading now** is a black card with a small bar (white = read, gold = the book you're on) and a white **Finished** button. **Up next** rows: name, then a soft gold **Vol. N** chip with the series bar (grey = read, gold = this volume, light = still to read); a series with more than 20 volumes shows a continuous bar with a gold dot. A/H badges are **blue**. The active tab has a **gold** line on top; the fair chip's icon is gold.
5. **Desktop `/queue`** — titles in black, "vol. N" in grey, the series bar under each title; location badges blue; a "Reading" status pill is blue (also on the series page and Books table).
6. **Gold legibility** — the gold chip text ("Vol. 6") and the gold bar segment are easy to read on the phone outdoors / at low brightness; the blue badges are not confused with links.
7. **Swipe right = finished** (phone Queue tab) — swipe an Up next row to the right: green "Finished" (a read book: "Re-read") appears behind it, the word only once there is room for it; past the threshold the sheet opens straight on _When did you finish?_ with Today selected → **Mark read** → the book leaves Up next and the toast offers the next volume. Start the swipe on the row, not at the screen edge (Android's back gesture lives there). Swipe left still removes.
8. **Find search** — the search box sits on a plain Stone strip across the full width (no white box around it); scroll the watchlist up: rows slide under the strip cleanly.
9. **Console** — the scope bar, preview table and the Add / Bulk Edit panel form one white card; no tiles behind the table or the form.
10. **Font** — every screen (iPad, phone, PC, laptop) shows the same Google Sans for English and Thai: rounder letters than before, Thai titles the same weight as the English ones. Numbers (Vol. 10, tiles, prices, "next 16") are Google Sans too, no longer typewriter-style, and still line up in columns (Books table Vol., series page). On the first open over a slow connection the text may flash in the system font for a moment. _Red flag:_ a Thai title in a different-looking font from its neighbours, or a box of squares.
11. **Scrolling** (phone, iPad) — scroll the Books table / a long Up next / the pile quickly: no stutter compared with before (the tiles are one 26×56 image repeated, so there should be none).

Report per device: OK, or what looked off (tiles stronger/weaker, a card with lines behind it, gold too pale/dark). Anything here is a quick token change before the deploy.
