- **Status:** ✅ Passed 2026-10-23 (your phone trial, no fixes) → deployed the same day
- **Feature:** STATUS.md → "Fair round 2 plan" → ② Extras (built 2026-10-23); branch `feature/extras`; mock-ups at https://claude.ai/artifact/1cBn2vSUw3aDj4jpa8CeQy (your pick: **A + Option 2**)
- **Setup:** `node .claude/dev-test-db.mjs --host` on the laptop (scratch DB, migration `0004` already applied there, port 5174), phone at `http://<laptop-IP>:5174/field`. Adding on scratch is safe; remove test books with Undo. The examples use **Silent Witch** (own 1–9 on scratch, extras after vol. 4 and vol. 9).

1. **+ Extra tile** — Find → Silent Witch: after **+ Vol. 10** there's a gold dashed tile **+ Extra**. It's not there while _Select several_ is on.
2. **The sheet** — tap it → "Add an extra · Silent Witch". **Comes after vol. 9** (the latest you own), "between vol. 9 and 10". − / + move it; at 0 it says "before vol. 1". One empty **Title** row; the price is the one from Find; Apartment / Home.
3. **A set** — tap **+** to vol. 10 → a gold box **"Add vol. 10 too — You don't have it yet — bought as a set?"**, ticked. Type `รวมตอนสั้น`, tap **Another extra after vol. 10**, type `รวมตอนพิเศษ` → the button reads **Save · vol. 10 + 2 extras**. Untick the box → **Save · 2 extras**; tick it again.
4. **Save** — toast **"Added vol. 10 + รวมตอนสั้น, รวมตอนพิเศษ"** · Silent Witch · Undo. The grid now has vol. 10 and the next is vol. 11. Scroll to **Extras**: **รวมตอนสั้น** [EXTRA] after vol. 10 and **รวมตอนพิเศษ** [EXTRA] after vol. 10, after the two untitled ones ("[EXTRA] after vol. 4", "[EXTRA] after vol. 9"). _Red flag:_ the two new extras look the same, or show "10.5".
5. **Undo** — add again and tap **Undo** in the toast → "Removed vol. 10 + รวมตอนสั้น, รวมตอนพิเศษ", all three gone. Add them once more for the next steps.
6. **Used before** — open + Extra again: under the title row, **Used before:** chips with the titles you typed. A chip fills the empty row (and leaves the chips). Close without saving.
7. **Reading sheet** — tap the **รวมตอนสั้น** chip → the sheet is titled "Silent Witch รวมตอนสั้น". **Add to reading queue**.
8. **Queue** — Queue tab → the row reads **Silent Witch · รวมตอนสั้น** with the gold **Extra** chip and the series bar. **Add a book to the queue** → Silent Witch's group lists the extras as "รวมตอนพิเศษ [EXTRA]". Remove the queued one again.
9. **Hitlist Bought** — swipe a watchlist row left → the Bought sheet has a gold **+ Extra** chip after "+ more". Tap it → an **Extras · come after** box (after the highest volume picked), title row, Used before chips → **Save · vol. N + 1 extra** → toast "Added vol. N + <title> · saved as draft" → Undo.
10. **Desktop** — on the PC (same server): Silent Witch's series page lists **รวมตอนสั้น [EXTRA] after vol. 10**. ✏️ on the untitled extra after vol. 9 → **Edit Extra** has a **Title** field → type one → Save → the row shows it. **Add Volume** (series page) → switch **Extra** on → the sort position fills itself with the next free slot (e.g. 10.7) and a **Title** field appears.
11. **Elsewhere** — Books table, the bookfair page (มหกรรมหนังสือระดับชาติ 31 on scratch) and the dashboard's Recent additions show titled extras by title + EXTRA. _Red flag:_ a raw "10.5" anywhere.
